## What's Changed

- Terminals now have a scrollbar. It follows your Ghostty `scrollbar` setting, matches a light or dark terminal background, and appears when you hover over the right edge if your Mac is set to always show scroll bars.
- Terminal ▸ Scroll to Top (⌘↑) and Scroll to Bottom (⌘↓) jump to either end of the scrollback.
- When you've scrolled up, a ↓ button appears at the bottom-right of the terminal; click it to jump back to the latest output.
- After you send a snippet from the sidebar, its context menu or the snippet editor, the keyboard goes back to the terminal, so you can keep typing without clicking first.
